PROJECT 01 / PREDICTIVE ANALYTICS

Understanding why customers leave.

Exploring customer lifecycle and usage patterns to understand retention risk, then translating those patterns into a machine learning pipeline.

PROJECT
Customer Churn Prediction & Behavioral Analysis
TOOLKIT
Python · Scikit-learn · Feature engineering

The question.

What can customer behavior tell us about the risk of churn?

Churn analysis brings together a business question and a modeling problem: understanding what distinguishes customers who leave from those who stay. This project explores customer lifecycle data and usage behaviors to identify patterns related to retention.

The approach.

  • Analyzed customer lifecycle and usage behavior to investigate factors associated with churn.
  • Engineered features and developed a classification pipeline to identify higher-risk customer segments.
  • Evaluated the predictive approach using precision, recall, and ROC-AUC.

The thinking.

The useful question goes beyond whether a classifier predicts churn. Precision and recall describe different types of errors, and those tradeoffs matter when deciding where to direct retention efforts. ROC-AUC offers another view of how the model separates the classes.

Scope & context.

This is an analytical and predictive modeling project. It explores signals that may support retention decisions; it does not establish that a particular intervention will prevent churn.

A wider perspective.

Behavioral analysis and model evaluation need to be considered together. A useful prediction starts with a well-defined question and an understanding of what an error means.
