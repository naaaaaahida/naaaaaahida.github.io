AI · DOCUMENT RETRIEVAL

Bangla PDF RAG.

OCR, multilingual embeddings, and retrieval-assisted answers for questions about Bangla PDFs.

PROJECT TYPE
Local AI application
TOOLKIT
Python · Streamlit · Tesseract · LangChain · FAISS
Source code (opens in a new tab)

The question.

How can the information inside a scanned Bangla document become easier to ask questions about?

The Streamlit app accepts a PDF, converts its pages to images, and uses Tesseract’s Bengali OCR to extract text. It then connects document retrieval with language generation.

The implementation.

  • Converted PDF pages at 150 DPI and extracted Bengali text with Tesseract.
  • Split text into overlapping chunks and indexed multilingual-e5-base embeddings in FAISS.
  • Retrieved six relevant chunks for a query and passed their context to a Groq-hosted language model through LangChain.

Design decisions.

OCR makes scanned PDFs usable as text. The retrieval stage narrows the material passed to the language model, while the prompt asks it to ground its answer in that context.

Scope & next steps.

Retrieval and prompting do not guarantee factual answers. OCR quality, chunk selection, document length, and the absence of source citations limit the current application. The repository’s Windows-oriented OCR/Poppler paths also need local configuration.
